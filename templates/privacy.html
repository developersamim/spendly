Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical information — basic data such as browser type and session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, and summarise your expenses.
  • Keep the service secure and working properly.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service.

We do not sell your personal information or expense data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Cookies

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your session. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Spendly and its users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including hashing passwords. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Your Rights

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may view, edit, or delete your expenses at any time, and you may delete your account, after which your personal data will be removed from our systems.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the Spendly team.
